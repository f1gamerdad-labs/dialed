Quick start

Your first five minutes.

The first time you open Dialed it walks you through a short tour. Here is what to expect.

1

Open Dialed first, then your sim

Dialed needs Windows permission to measure your frame rate, so Windows asks when it opens. Open Dialed before you start your sim: that prompt can freeze a full-screen game. Set your game to Borderless Windowed so it stays on screen when you click another monitor.

2

Pick your sim

iRacing and Le Mans Ultimate today, with the rest coming soon. Dialed finds your games for you, and each sim keeps its own settings table and its own history.

3

Capture three runs

Start your game and press the hotkey while you drive. You get a spoken or beeped countdown, then 60 seconds are recorded. Do it three times in the same session, with a full grid on your heaviest track if you can, or let Dialed run all three for you.

4

Get a short priority list

Dialed works out whether your processor, graphics card or graphics memory is the limit, then lists the changes best first with their gain, visual cost and how sure it is. The top three are ticked for you.

5

Apply safely, then check

One click backs up your settings file, closes the sim after a big warning, and writes the change. Start the game, capture again, and Dialed tells you what each change did and what to put back.

6

And there is more to explore

The PC check finds problems outside the sim. Results draws your frame times and marks every stutter. Save profiles, share a before/after image, and tell Dialed how you race under About your setup.

How it works

Capture. Read. Change. Compare.

No guessing and no copying someone else's settings. Everything is worked out from your own frame data and your own settings file.

1

Capture

Press a hotkey while you drive. Dialed records about a minute of frame data with Intel PresentMon, which it includes. Do it three times for firmer advice, or let Dialed run three in a row for you.

2

Diagnose

It works out your average FPS, your 1% lows and stutters, and whether the processor, graphics card or graphics memory is the limit.

3

Recommend

A short priority list, best first. Each setting shows its gain, its visual cost, plain-English what you will see change, and how confident Dialed is. The top three are ticked for you.

4

Apply safely

One click backs up your file into a visible Dialed Backups folder and writes the changes. If the sim is open, a big warning tells you it will close first.

5

Compare

Capture again. Dialed remembers each round, tells you what every change did, and suggests what to put back if something made it worse.

6

Undo any time

Restore any backup from inside the app. Your current file is backed up first, so even a restore can be undone.

The priority list: what to change, how much load it takes off, how much you will notice it, and how sure Dialed is.

Built to be trusted

Rules, not a chatbot.

There is no AI running on your PC and nothing is sent anywhere to get your advice. Dialed uses a table of what each setting costs and what it looks like, checked against recorded captures, and it says plainly when it is only estimating.

  • Private. Your captures and files stay on your PC.
  • Honest. Settings it has not been able to verify are marked low confidence.
  • Careful. It only touches the graphics settings it lists, and keeps everything else in your file exactly as it was.
  • Updates inside the app, never while you are driving, and always your choice.

Help

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

Dialed is not signed with a paid code-signing certificate yet, so Windows shows this for any new app. Click More info, then Run anyway. The full source is public on GitHub if you want to check what it does.

Does it change my game files?

Only when you press Apply, only the graphics settings it lists, and always after saving a backup. Backups live in a visible Dialed Backups folder next to the sim's own settings, and you can restore any of them in the app.

Why does it close my sim?

Sims rewrite their settings file when they exit, which would overwrite the change. So Dialed warns you clearly, closes the sim, saves the file, and you start the sim again.

Which sim should I test with, and where?

Pick the heaviest session your PC handles: a full grid, ideally in rain or at night, and capture the first lap. The Setup page lists suggested tests for each sim, and a Launch button starts the game through Steam with Dialed already open. Use the same session every time so rounds compare fairly.

Does it work in VR?

Not for VR. Dialed is built for monitors, single screen and triples, and it will not accept a VR settings file. For VR, I'd suggest using NextLevelXR, which is made for it.

Something went wrong.

Use the Report button in the app. It saves a zip with your log and (if you tick them) your captures and graphics settings, and sends your message to me. The zip is not uploaded by the app.

Does it send anything about me?

No. The only network use is checking GitHub for updates (which you can switch off) and the Report and Ideas buttons when you press them. There is also an optional Share my result button that shows you exactly what it would send and only sends it if you press Send. The more results people share, the more confident the recommendations can become over time. If you want to help improve the advice without pressing it every time, you can tick "share automatically" there (or in About). Then each finished round sends the same anonymous summary: the sim, what limited your PC, the settings changed with their confidence, whether each change was kept, the before and after frame rates and, if you ran the PC check, your processor, graphics card, memory and screen count. No name, email, file paths or capture files. It is off until you turn it on, and you can switch it off at any time.

What do you do with my email address?

You give it to get the Dialed download link. It goes into my email list (run by Kit), and I use it to send you Dialed updates and the occasional F1GamerDad sim racing email. You can unsubscribe in one click from any email. I don't sell it or share it. The app never sees your email, and it is never linked to results you choose to share.

Where do I send ideas?

Use the Ideas button in the app, or the form it opens.
