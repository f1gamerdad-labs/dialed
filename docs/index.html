How it works

Capture. Read. Change. Compare.

No guessing and no copying someone else's settings. Everything is worked out from your own frame data and your own settings file.

1

Capture

Press a hotkey while you drive. Dialed records about a minute of frame data with Intel PresentMon, which it includes. Do it two or three times for firmer advice.

2

Diagnose

It works out your average FPS, your 1% lows and stutters, and whether the processor, graphics card or graphics memory is the limit.

3

Recommend

A short priority list, best first. Each setting shows its gain, its visual cost, plain-English what you will see change, and how confident Dialed is. The top three are ticked for you.

4

Apply safely

One click backs up your file into a visible Dialed Backups folder and writes the changes. If the sim is open, a big warning tells you it will close first.

5

Compare

Capture again. Dialed remembers each round, tells you what every change did, and suggests what to put back if something made it worse.

6

Undo any time

Restore any backup from inside the app. Your current file is backed up first, so even a restore can be undone.

The priority list: what to change, how much load it takes off, how much you will notice it, and how sure Dialed is.

Built to be trusted

Rules, not a chatbot.

There is no AI running on your PC and nothing is sent anywhere to get your advice. Dialed uses a table of what each setting costs and what it looks like, checked against recorded captures, and it says plainly when it is only estimating.

  • Private. Your captures and files stay on your PC.
  • Honest. Settings it has not been able to verify are marked low confidence.
  • Careful. It only touches the graphics settings it lists, and keeps everything else in your file exactly as it was.
  • Updates inside the app, never while you are driving, and always your choice.

More than a settings list

Everything around the settings, too.

Some of the biggest frame rate problems are not in the sim at all. Dialed looks at those as well, and makes the whole process easier to repeat and to share.

PC check

Finds memory running below its rated speed (XMP or EXPO off), the wrong power plan, a screen below its top refresh rate, screens plugged into the motherboard, a graphics card on fewer lanes, and more. It tells you what to do about each one.

Frame times

See your capture over time with every stutter marked and a plain-language reading of what is causing it.

Fits how you race

Tell Dialed how your screen syncs, whether you stream, and which settings it must never touch. Your screen's refresh rate is the default target.

Saved profiles

Save your settings as "Daytona" or "Rain" and put them back with one click, with a backup first.

Three runs in a row

Press once and Dialed records three runs back to back, with spoken cues in F1GamerDad's voice or plain beeps, your choice.

Share your result

Make a before/after image of your gain for Discord or a comment. It is made on your PC and nothing is uploaded.

Get it

Download and install

1. Download

Dialed-win-x64.zip (63 MB) from this page. All releases are on GitHub.

2. Unzip

Right-click the zip, choose Extract All, and put the folder somewhere permanent, like Documents. Do not run it from inside the zip.

3. Open it first

Run Dialed.exe before you start your sim. It asks for administrator rights because PresentMon needs them, and that prompt can freeze a full-screen game.

Help

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

Dialed is not signed with a paid code-signing certificate yet, so Windows shows this for any new app. Click More info, then Run anyway. The full source is public on GitHub if you want to check what it does.

Does it change my game files?

Only when you press Apply, only the graphics settings it lists, and always after saving a backup. Backups live in a visible Dialed Backups folder next to the sim's own settings, and you can restore any of them in the app.

Why does it close my sim?

Sims rewrite their settings file when they exit, which would overwrite the change. So Dialed warns you clearly, closes the sim, saves the file, and you start the sim again.

Which sim should I test with, and where?

Pick the heaviest session your PC handles: a full grid, ideally in rain or at night, and capture the first lap. The Setup page lists suggested tests for each sim, and a Launch button starts the game through Steam with Dialed already open. Use the same session every time so rounds compare fairly.

Does it work in VR?

Not for VR. Dialed is built for monitors, single screen and triples, and it will not accept a VR settings file. For VR, I'd suggest using NextLevelXR, which is made for it.

Something went wrong.

Use the Report button in the app. It saves a zip with your log and (if you tick them) your captures and graphics settings, and sends your message to me. The zip is not uploaded by the app.

Does it send anything about me?

No. The only network use is checking GitHub for updates (which you can switch off) and the Report and Ideas buttons when you press them. There is also an optional Share my result button that shows you exactly what it would send and only sends it if you press Send.

Where do I send ideas?

Use the Ideas button in the app, or the form it opens.
