      Capture

      Before you capture, close as many other programs as you can. That means OBS, Chrome and other browsers, Discord, overlays, and anything running in the system tray (check the arrow by the clock). Background programs are the most common cause of stutter and they skew your results.

      Open this app before you start the sim. Windows asks for permission when the app opens, and that prompt can freeze a full-screen game. Once the app is open, you never see it again, and the hotkey works from inside the game.

      Click Start capture, or press the hotkey while you are in the sim. You get a 10 second countdown (spoken or beeps, your choice), then 60 seconds are recorded, then a sound to say it is done. Drive (or watch a replay) with the same track, car and field each time.

                About

                Version and updates

                Updates come from this project's GitHub releases only. Checking sends nothing about you or your PC, and an update is never installed without you pressing Update now.

                Appearance and accessibility

                Colour-blind friendly colours use blue and magenta instead of green and pink for good and bad, and yellow for caution. They were checked against the three common types of colour blindness. The wording never relies on colour alone: results say Better or Worse, and changes show a + or -. High contrast buttons make every button one solid deep purple instead of the orange to pink gradient, so the white text is easier to read.

                Window and system tray

                The tray is the row of small icons by the clock (it may be behind the arrow). Right-click the Dialed icon there to open it, start or stop a capture, or exit. The capture hotkey and the sounds work the same while it is hidden. With the first option off, closing the window closes Dialed.

                Move to a new PC, or keep a copy

                Export saves your rounds, profiles and preferences into one file in Documents\Dialed\Exports. Copy it to the new PC and use Import there. Your captures and your games' own settings files are not included. Nothing is uploaded.

                Dialed by F1GamerDad

                Sim graphics, dialed.

                Settings built from your own PC, not copied from someone else's. It records your frame data with PresentMon and reads your sim's graphics settings, works out whether your processor, graphics card or graphics memory is holding you back, and suggests the changes that help most for the least visual cost.

                Not for VR. Dialed is built for monitors, single screen and triples. For VR, I'd suggest using NextLevelXR, which is made for it.

                Private. Everything runs on your PC. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share a result, and then only the anonymous numbers you are shown (no name, file paths or email). Sharing automatically is off unless you turn it on.

                Small steps. Apply a few changes, capture again, and the tool tells you what each round did and what to put back.

                Frame capture uses Intel PresentMon, released under the MIT licence. Made by F1GamerDad.
